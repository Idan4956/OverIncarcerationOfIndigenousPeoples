Understanding justice in Canada

Indigenous justice, The Truth of Life

Indigenous peoples continue to be overrepresented in Canada's criminal justice system. This project looks at the history, systems, and human stories behind that reality, and at the work toward change.

Why does this happen?

Circumstances only start it, Systems perpetuate it.

Why has it happened? Substance abuse, family violence, and historical trauma contribute to the overrepresentation of Indigenous peoples in the justice system.

What perpetuates this cycle? Indigenous women feel undervalued within the justice and societal systems. Incarcerated indigenous people are subject to cultural disconnection.
